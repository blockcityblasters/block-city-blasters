Buttons

💥 FIREShoots your weapon (auto-aims). In a car: car blasters. In the chopper: rockets.
📦 AmmoAmmo is NOT unlimited! The count is on the FIRE button. Find olive-and-yellow ammo crates (orange squares on the map). Robots sometimes drop them, bosses always do, and HQ has a crate that comes back every 45 seconds. Chopper rockets reload when you land at HQ.
👥 FriendsPlay together! Each person opens this same game on their own phone, tablet or computer at the same time (signed in to Claude, with the game shared to them). You'll see each other run, drive and fly, with name tags, and on the map. Your shots help each other beat robots. Set your name in Shop → Avatar.
💬 TalkWhen friends are playing, tap 💬 Talk and pick a picture (👋 Hi!, 🙌 Follow me!, 🆘 Help me!…). Your friend sees a bubble over your head and hears it read out loud.
🌦 SurprisesRandom weather (rain makes roads slippery, thunderstorms, fog) and random events: 🛸 UFO attacks, 🦾 a giant Titan robot, ☄️ meteor showers (run from the red circles, grab the space crystals). Find 4 landmarks (stadium, airport, theme park with a Ferris wheel, lighthouse) for +50 coins each, and hunt for 10 hidden 🗿 golden statues (some are on rooftops!) to unlock special hats.
🎯 MissionsMissions are glowing beams with a picture on top (also on the map). Walk or drive into one — a talking helper explains it with a moving picture — then press ▶ GO! 13 kinds: Robot Attack, Zombie Horde, Speed Delivery, Star Hunt, Giant Boss, 🏁 Street Race (drive through the gates), 🏢 Rooftop Rescue, 🛡 Defend HQ (protect the core), 💣 Bomb Squad (stand on bombs to defuse), 🪙 Coin Rush, 🚁 Rooftop Tanks, 💥 Demolition Derby and ✨ Golden Robot (it runs away — catch it for lots of coins!).
🏢 BuildingsOrange buildings on the map (yellow door) can be entered! Step on the glowing ⬆ pads to go up floor by floor. Each floor is furnished (lobby, offices, an apartment, storage) and has coins, ammo and enemies. Shoot or blow up the furniture! The ROOF has a 💰 treasure chest (coins + full ammo). Drop through the hole to go down. Enemies can follow you inside!
🗺 MapBlock City is an island! A curvy coastal highway loops all the way around it. Downtown skyscrapers in the middle, a river with bridges, houses with yards, an industrial zone and beaches on the coast. The whole map is open — explore it all. Earn XP (yellow bar under your health) from missions and beating enemies to level up (tougher enemies & bosses). Getting zapped sends you back to Level 1 — but you keep your coins and weapons.
👾 BossesBigger and tougher every level. LV3+: calls backup robots. LV5+: ground-slam shockwave (jump!). LV6+: gets ENRAGED at half health. LV8+: energy shield 🛡 (wait for it to drop).
💥 Smash!Drive into lamp posts, trees, fire hydrants (water spray!), trash cans, benches, mailboxes and cones to knock them flying. Smashing stuff sometimes drops coins. Fast cars even plow through built blocks.
🔥 VehiclesCars and the chopper have their own health bar. Damaged: smoke 💨 → fire 🔥 → beeping countdown → BOOM! Jump out before it blows (and run!). Landing the chopper at HQ repairs it.
🔫 WeaponSwitch between the weapons you own: Blaster, Shotgun, Laser Rifle, Rocket Launcher, Mini-Gun.
✈ AirstrikeKillstreak! Beat 12 enemies in a row without getting zapped (zombies count as half) and a jet bombs the closest enemies. Getting zapped resets your streak.
🤝 BuddyTurns the nearest robot into your friend for 90 seconds. Recharges every 4 enemies (max 3).
🚗🚁 In/OutGet in or out of a car or the helicopter. Cars stop when you walk up. Jumping out of the chopper up high opens a parachute 🪂.
🚁 HelicopterParked at HQ (the yellow H on the map). Stick: forward/turn. Hold ⬆ Jump to go UP, hold ⬇ Down to go down.
🏃 RunPush the stick all the way out, or tap 🏃 Run (stays on until you tap it again). On a computer hold Shift. The cyan bar under your health is stamina — when it runs out, you have to catch your breath. Sprint Shoes in the Shop let you run faster and longer.
⬆ JumpJump. In a car with Nitro: speed blast! With Rocket Boots: hold in the air to fly.
🧱 BuildFIRE becomes PLACE. 🎨 changes color, 🔨 breaks a block.
❤ DamageThe red edge shows where you got hit from. Blue armor takes hits first. Health comes back after a few seconds without getting hit. Big falls and hard car crashes hurt!
